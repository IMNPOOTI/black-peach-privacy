Dealer's Choice Poker

Privacy policy · 18 November 2026

01 / Privacy policy · effective 18 November 2026

Play poker. Keep control of your data.

Dealer's Choice Poker is a private, play-money poker game for our Discord community. This policy explains what information the game keeps, how it is used, what other participants can see and what choices you have inside the application.

What we collect

  • Discord identity: your Discord account ID and name when you sign in.
  • Poker records: tournament registration names, game and tournament history, results, chip and prize records, and player statistics.
  • Account and security information: the session and security information needed to operate the game and reconnect players.
  • Optional profile features: avatar, spoken-name recordings and aliases, items, emojis and personal game settings, when you choose to use them.
  • Browser information: cookies used for sign-in; your browser may also store display preferences.

How information is used

We use this information to run tournaments, reconnect players, display results, provide optional features and maintain account security.

Information visible to other players

Other players and authorised spectators can see normal public game information, including your tournament name, avatar, public actions, chip stack and results.

Your career statistics are visible to other players by default. You can turn Show My Statistics off in the game's Settings. You and the room owner can still access those statistics, and this setting does not hide public tournament history.

Retention, history and backups

We keep account information while your account is in use. Shared tournament and hand history may be retained indefinitely so other players' results remain accurate. If you request account deletion, retained shared records are anonymised rather than removed from everyone's historical results.

If enabled, application-managed encrypted backups are kept for up to seven days. The application includes safeguards against restoring erased account information from those backups. It cannot remove copies independently saved by other people or held by external services.

External services

Discord provides sign-in. Cloudflare may handle access and network traffic where configured, and hosting providers may process connection information. These providers may process information under their own privacy practices.

02 / Your choices

Manage your data in-game.

You can review your available information in your player profile and Settings. The game also provides privacy controls and a way to request permanent account deletion.

Career statistics visibility

Open Settings → Show My Statistics to control whether other players can view your career statistics. Turning it off does not hide public tournament history, and you and the room owner retain access.

Request permanent account deletion

In the game, go to Settings → Request Permanent Account Deletion.

The room owner reviews and carries out your request. Deletion removes your account-linked identity, personal statistics, preferences, avatar and managed spoken-name recordings. Shared historical records remain with your identity anonymised. Deletion may need to wait until a tournament involving you has finished.

Deletion is a request, not an instant action. The room owner reviews and processes it, while preserving anonymised shared game history.

Questions, access and corrections

For access or correction requests, privacy questions or complaints, contact the room owner through The Black Peach Discord Community. The owner will review your request and respond through Discord.

You can also use the website's privacy contact: blackpeach-privacy@proton.me.
