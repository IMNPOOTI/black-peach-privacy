Application 01 / 07

Hooves of Thunder

Discord authentication

Discord sign-in for the horse racing website. We check server membership and roles to control access, with minimal data retention.

Privacy policy · 9 October 2026

01 / Privacy policy · effective 9 October 2026

Only the information needed to sign in.

Hooves of Thunder uses Discord OAuth2 to authenticate visitors to the horse racing website. This Discord application is solely an authentication service: it checks identity, membership of the required Discord server, and the required role. It is not a chat bot.

Information processed

When you choose to sign in with Discord, the authentication service can process your Discord account ID, basic profile information returned by Discord (such as username and avatar), and the server membership and role information necessary to decide whether access is allowed. The authorisation prompt requests:

01
Read your member information for servers you belong to

Used to verify membership and the relevant server role.

02
Access your username, avatar and banner

Basic Discord profile access used in the sign-in exchange. Not every available profile field needs to be retained.

We do not request Discord email addresses or permission to read messages, chat history or private conversations. Authentication information is not used for advertising or profiling.

How sign-in works

  1. 01You choose Discord sign-in and authorise the two requested permissions.
  2. 02The Aiko Discord OIDC Worker checks the necessary identity, server membership and role information with Discord and passes authentication claims to Cloudflare Access.
  3. 03Cloudflare Access allows or denies entry and uses an authentication session cookie to recognise your browser during the session.

Retention and storage

We have configured the Worker with role caching disabled (cacheRoles: false). Its Cloudflare KV store contains a cryptographic signing-key entry, not a stored database of Discord users, conversations or cached membership lists. The Worker processes sign-in information as needed for authentication.

Cloudflare Access is separate from the Worker’s KV storage. It uses necessary session cookies and generates authentication/security logs. Depending on the event and service configuration, those logs can include an identity reference, time of access, IP address and allow/deny outcome. Cloudflare retains this information in accordance with its applicable service and plan retention schedules. We do not maintain a separate archive of authentication logs.

Sharing and security

Authentication information is processed by Discord (identity provider) and Cloudflare (Worker and access control) to provide the sign-in service. We do not sell this information or disclose it for advertising. Access to our configuration and administrative controls is restricted.

02 / Your choices

Privacy and contact.

Using Discord sign-in is optional, but it is necessary to enter the Discord-protected game website.

Requests about your information

For questions, access, correction or deletion requests relating to this authentication application, contact:

blackpeach-privacy@proton.me

As the Worker does not maintain a stored user-profile or role database, we may have no separate Worker-held personal record to erase. We will review requests concerning information under our control. Discord and Cloudflare separately manage their own account, session and service records under their respective policies.

Ending a session

You can sign out of the protected website, clear its authentication cookies, or revoke the application's access from your Discord authorised-app settings. Existing sessions may persist until their configured expiry unless separately revoked.
