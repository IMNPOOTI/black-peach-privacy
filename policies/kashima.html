Kashima

UFC betting · virtual currency

Community UFC betting using fictional balances, fighter selections and leaderboards. No real-money payments are handled.

Privacy policy · 9 October 2026

01 / Privacy policy · effective 9 October 2026

Virtual bets. Clear records.

Kashima runs community UFC betting with fictional in-bot currency only. When you take part, the bot processes your Discord identity and betting activity so it can maintain balances, accept bets, settle wagers and display standings. It does not process bank details, payment cards or real-money deposits.

Information collected and stored

  • Discord account ID and display name: identify your account, associate your wagers with you, and show your name on betting boards and leaderboards. The stored display name is refreshed during certain interactions.
  • Virtual wallet: balance, debt, buy-ins, and the date your wallet record was created.
  • Straight bets: selected event and market, fighter or over/under selection, stake, odds, status and creation time.
  • Parlays: event and fighter selections for each leg, individual and combined odds, stake, payout, status, creation time and settlement time when applicable.
  • Virtual advances and repayments: records of advances (buy-ins), amounts and associated debt, plus changes to wallet and debt totals.
What Kashima does not do: It does not request the privileged Message Content Intent, monitor ordinary channel conversations or store a message archive. It does not collect payment or banking information.

Where information is kept

Participant records are saved in a local SQLite database (kashima.db) operated by the bot owner. The bot also keeps UFC event schedule data in a local cache; this schedule is not a database of Discord members.

Before certain administrative fight-closing operations, Kashima makes local database backups for recovery. Its code keeps up to five of the most recent database backup files; backups may contain earlier copies of account and betting records. The code does not set a maximum age for those backups.

When information is visible

Some information is deliberately shared in the Discord server as part of the game: public standings show display names, virtual wallet values and debt; closing a fight posts active betting tickets and parlay details, which may include names and stakes. Individual /wallet responses are private (ephemeral), while other betting announcements can appear publicly. Messages already sent to Discord are subject to Discord's own retention and deletion behaviour.

Third-party services

Kashima uses Discord to receive commands and display results. Its UFC event lookup requests public fight-card information from ESPN and caches that event information locally. The code does not send members' wallet, betting or Discord account records to ESPN.

Retention and protection

The current code does not automatically expire participant records when a bet finishes or a member leaves the server. Records remain in the local database until they are removed or the database is retired. Local backup copies must also be considered when handling deletion requests. Database files are not application-level encrypted by this script; protection depends on the machine's storage and access controls.

02 / Your choices

Your betting information.

You may use Kashima's commands to view your own virtual balance and pending wagers. Server members can also view the published standings and any publicly announced tickets.

Access, correction and deletion

To request a copy of the information held about you, correct an account association, or ask for your records to be removed, contact:

blackpeach-privacy@proton.me

Include your Discord user ID so we can identify the correct account. Requests are handled by the operator; the current bot has no built-in self-service deletion command. Where a request involves pending virtual bets, we may need to cancel or resolve those entries as part of deletion. We will also account for retained database backups.

Other information

The bot's use of Discord is also subject to Discord's privacy practices. UFC event schedules are obtained from ESPN; player betting records are not provided to that service.
