Privacy, without the mystery.

Each application has its own privacy page explaining what information it uses, what it retains, and how to request access to or deletion of personal data. We update these pages when an application’s features or data practices change.

Questions about your data?

For privacy enquiries or data-deletion requests, email blackpeach-privacy@proton.me. Please mention which application your request concerns so we can help you with the right information.
